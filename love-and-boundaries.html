PERSONAL GROWTH

You can love someone and still need boundaries.

Caring deeply about another person doesn’t require disappearing from your own life.

For some of us, saying no feels like letting someone down. We say yes because we love them, because they need us, or because conflict feels harder than exhaustion.

But a boundary isn’t a punishment. It’s a way to be honest about what you can offer without building resentment around what you cannot.

It might sound like: “I care about you, and I can listen tonight for twenty minutes. After that, I need to rest.” The words can be gentle and still be firm.

Healthy boundaries take practice. The goal isn’t to stop caring. It’s to make room for care that is sustainable for both people.

Every story deserves to be heard.
